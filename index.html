📋 Prerequisites & Student Eligibility

Important: These benefits are exclusively available to students who have an active GitHub Student Developer Pack or a verifiable university email address (e.g. .edu or .edu.ph).

🎓 Apply for GitHub Student Pack ☁️ Claim Azure for Students ($100 Free)
ℹ️
No Credit Card Required for Azure for Students! If you activate strictly through the student portal using your university email, Microsoft will not ask for a credit or debit card. Services simply pause if credits reach zero, ensuring 100% peace of mind.

Step-by-Step Activation Process:

1

Activate Your GitHub Student Developer Pack

Go to GitHub Education. Sign in with your GitHub account, submit proof of enrollment (Student ID, registration form, or school email), and wait for approval.

2

Go to Azure for Students Portal

Visit azure.microsoft.com/en-us/free/students and click the green "Start free" button.

3

Sign in with your University Email

Log in using your school email (e.g. @wmsu.edu.ph). When prompted by Microsoft, choose "Work or school account".

4

Academic Verification Complete

Microsoft verifies your academic status via institutional email or your GitHub Student Pack. Your subscription will be provisioned with $100 annual credits and 12 months of free popular services!

5

What if Azure asks for a card (Free Trial $200)?

If your student quota was already used or you choose the standard 30-day $200 Free Trial, Azure will require a debit card for identity verification. Keep at least ₱100 – ₱150 PHP in your digital bank (like MariBank, GCash Card, or Maya) to pass the temporary ~$1.00 USD authorization check.

🛡️ Step 2: Protect Your Card (Set Budget Alert)

Before launching any VM or database, always create a Cost Budget. This guarantees Microsoft alerts you before your credits run low.

⚠️
Never Skip This Step! If you linked a debit card (such as MariBank, GCash, or Maya), a budget alert ensures you never get billed unexpected charges after trial credits expire.
1

Open Cost Management

Search for Cost Management + Billing in the top search bar of the Azure Portal.

2

Create New Budget

Go to Budgets ➔ Click + Add. Set Budget Name as StudentBudget and Amount to $50.00 or $100.00.

3

Add Email Notifications

Enter your student or personal email address so Azure emails you when spend reaches 50%, 75%, and 90%.

💻 Creating Your Ubuntu Virtual Machine

For projects that use Laravel, WebSockets (Reverb), queue workers, or multiple APIs, deploying an Ubuntu VM gives total control.

1

VM Settings

Image: Ubuntu Server 24.04 LTS - x64 Gen2 (Make sure architecture is x64, not ARM64).

2

Recommended Sizing

• Standard_B1s (1 vCPU, 1 GB RAM) ➔ 100% Free for 12 months.
• Standard_B2as_v2 (2 vCPU, 8 GB RAM) ➔ ~$38/month (Covered by your $200 trial credits).

3

Open Inbound Ports

In Networking / Firewall rules, allow: Port 22 (SSH), Port 80 (HTTP), Port 443 (HTTPS), and Port 8080 (WebSockets).

CONNECT VIA SSH
ssh -i /path/to/your_key.pem azureuser@<YOUR_PUBLIC_IP>

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Will my debit card be charged automatically?

Only if you exceed your $200 credit and free-tier quotas on a Pay-As-You-Go subscription. Having a Budget Alert ensures you get warned before charges occur.

Q: Do I need to buy a custom domain?

No! Azure provides free *.azurestaticapps.net domains with HTTPS. You also get free .me domains from the GitHub Student Developer Pack via Namecheap.

Q: Can multiple students share one VM?

Yes! An 8 GB RAM Ubuntu VM can host multiple Laravel APIs, React frontends, and databases using Nginx subdomains.
